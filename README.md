# Seneca Commons

Free financial calculators and signal tools for uncertain times.

**[senecacommons.com](https://senecacommons.com)**

---

## Tools

**US — Cost of Living & Relocation**
- [Moving Cost Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/moving-cost-calculator) — cross-country move estimate by distance, home size, season
- [Best Cities to Retire on a Fixed Income](https://senecacommons.com/tools/best-cities-to-retire-calculator) — ranks US cities by purchasing power, state tax treatment, climate
- [Remote Work Salary Adjustment](https://senecacommons.com/tools/remote-work-salary-adjustment-calculator) — what your salary buys in a different city
- [Cost of Living by State](https://senecacommons.com/tools/cost-of-living-by-state-calculator) — state-level COL and tax comparison

**US — Finance**
- [FIRE Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/early-retirement-fire-calculator) — FIRE number and retirement age with adjustable safe withdrawal rate
- [Debt-to-Income Ratio Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/debt-to-income-ratio-calculator) — front-end and back-end DTI against Conventional/FHA/VA thresholds
- [EV Cost Savings Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/electric-vehicle-cost-savings-calculator) — 5-year total cost, gas vs electric

**India — Finance**
- [GST Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/gst-calculator-india) — CGST/SGST/IGST split with slab table
- [GST Easy Calculation](https://senecacommons.com/tools/gst-calculator-easy-calculation) — single-step GST lookup
- [SIP Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/sip-calculator-india) — projected corpus with inflation adjustment
- [Salary Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/salary-bonus-calculator-india) — CTC to in-hand breakdown
- [Payroll Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/payroll-calculator-india-excel) — employer cost view
- [Salary Budget Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/salary-budget-calculator-india) — monthly spending plan from take-home pay, 50/30/20 or custom split
- [Salary Difference Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/salary-difference-calculator-india) — compare two CTC offers, gross and in-hand, with tax-slab impact
- [Retirement Calculator India](https://senecacommons.com/tools/retirement-calculator-india) — retirement corpus with EPF, NPS and inflation-adjusted projections

**Prediction Markets**
- [Implied Probability Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/implied-probability-calculator)
- [Kelly Criterion Calculator](https://senecacommons.com/tools/kelly-criterion-calculator)

## Signal

[Seneca Sentinel](https://senecacommons.com/signal/sentinel) — six-panel macroeconomic and systemic risk dashboard covering macro indicators, immiseration data, market triggers, local conditions, and a real-time Fear/Greed index across Polymarket categories.

[Kalshi vs Polymarket](https://senecacommons.com/signal/kalshi-vs-polymarket) — comparison of the two major US prediction market platforms.
